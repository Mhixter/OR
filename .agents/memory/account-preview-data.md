---
name: Account preview data
description: Rules for illustrative merchant account values, history, and QR content.
---

Illustrative merchant account data must remain visibly differentiated from persisted workspace records, which retain their own factual values and local references.

**Why:** The console does not connect to a production wallet account, so preview balance/history must not be mistaken for real transactions or production references.

**How to apply:** Keep current-month history separate when merging it with saved records, preserve saved event fields, and label preview account balances, history, receipts, and QR context wherever they appear.
